About

One person between the server room, the business and the browser.

My work sits where operations meet software. On one side I manage identities and devices with Microsoft Azure, Entra ID and Intune, keep infrastructure healthy and resolve the hardest support cases. On the other, I map business processes and turn them into Odoo implementations, custom tools and websites.

Outside the office I run my own private cloud: virtualized servers, containerized services, encrypted backups and secure remote access — the same principles I apply for clients, at home scale.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Information Technology from Universidad de Guadalajara (CUCEA) and a technical degree in Software Development from CETI.
