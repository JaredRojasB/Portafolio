Infrastructure · Case study

A private cloud built from hardware that was already in a drawer.

Photos, documents and a family movie library moved off paid cloud storage and onto reused computers at home — virtualized, backed up every night, reachable from anywhere and never exposed to the internet.

01 · The goal

Stop paying for storage without giving up convenience.

The family's photos lived in a paid cloud subscription that kept growing, movies were scattered across laptops and nobody knew where the important documents were. The brief was simple: one private place for everything, as easy as the apps they already used.

    02 · Architecture

    Three reused machines, one job each.

    A main node runs every service in isolated containers; a low‑power node receives encrypted backups and watches the others; a third machine stays on standby for heavier tasks.
